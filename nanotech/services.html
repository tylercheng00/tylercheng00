業務領域

Services

技術轉移、研發諮詢、專利佈局與共同開發——為科研與產業夥伴提供一站式轉化支援。

Technology transfer, R&D consulting, patent strategy and co-development — end-to-end support for research and industry partners.

01

技術轉移 Technology Transfer

Technology Transfer

我們協助把實驗室成果轉化為可授權、可落地、可融資的技術資產。服務對象包括高校研究組、初創團隊、企業創新部門，以及尋求外部技術導入的製造商。

We help turn laboratory results into licensable, executable and investable technology assets — for university groups, startups, corporate innovation teams and manufacturers seeking inbound technology.

  • 技術成熟度（TRL）評估與差距分析
  • Technology readiness (TRL) assessment and gap analysis
  • 商業化路徑與合作模式設計（授權、合資、共同開發）
  • Commercialisation pathway and deal-structure design (licence, JV, co-dev)
  • 技術說明書、演示包與潛在被授權方對接
  • Technical briefs, demo packages and licensee outreach

03

專利申請協助

Patent filing support

我們協助發明人與機構把技術構想整理為可申請的知識產權資產，並就香港、中國內地等司法管轄區的佈局提出建議。我們可協調代理人網絡，並以自身專利實務經驗支援交底與策略討論。

We help inventors and organisations turn technical ideas into fileable IP assets, advising on Hong Kong, mainland China and related jurisdictions. We can coordinate agent networks and support disclosures with our own patenting experience.

  • 發明披露梳理與可專利性分析（高層次）
  • Invention disclosure structuring and high-level patentability discussion
  • 申請策略：地區、類型（發明／新型）、時序
  • Filing strategy: geography, type (invention / utility model), timing
  • 與代理人、翻譯及技術圖紙協調
  • Coordination with agents, translation and technical drawings
04

共同研發新技術

Collaborative R&D

類似香港應用納米及先進材料研發院（NAMI）所代表的「產學研共創」模式，我們歡迎與夥伴聯合開發新技術：從概念驗證、原型迭代、工藝放大，到共同知識產權安排。

In the spirit of applied research platforms such as NAMI in Hong Kong, we co-develop new technologies with partners — from proof-of-concept and prototype iteration to process scale-up and shared IP arrangements.

  • 聯合項目定義與里程碑管理
  • Joint project definition and milestone management
  • 材料／裝置原型與工藝驗證支援
  • Materials/device prototyping and process validation support
  • 知識產權歸屬與商業化條款協商框架
  • Frameworks for IP ownership and commercialisation terms
