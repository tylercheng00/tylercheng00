定位Focus

人工智能遇上生物科技

Where AI meets biotechnology

BTRDCC 專注於人工智能與生物科技的結合。其技術方向把納米標靶與癌症治療技術研發結合：以 AI 分析生物數據、篩選納米載體設計，令後續實驗工作集中在最有潛力的方案上，並配合知識產權保護。

BTRDCC works where artificial intelligence meets biotechnology. Its direction combines nano-targeting with cancer-therapy technology research: AI helps analyse biological data and narrow nano-carrier designs so laboratory work focuses on the most promising candidates — with IP protection in parallel.
