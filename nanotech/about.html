About us

納米科研技術有限公司與康納科研技術（廣州）有限公司，共同構建「香港知識產權與國際合作 × 廣州研發落地」的雙引擎。

Nanotech Innovation Limited and Kangna Research Technology (Guangzhou) form a dual engine: Hong Kong IP and international collaboration, plus Guangzhou R&D execution.

使命Mission

讓科研成果找到可規模化的路徑

Give research a scalable path

我們相信，優秀的科學需要同時具備工程可行性、知識產權保護與產業夥伴網絡。公司定位接近應用科研平台（如 NAMI 等機構所代表的技術轉化角色）：不是純學術發表，而是把技術帶到可用、可保護、可合作的狀態。

Great science also needs engineering feasibility, IP protection and industry networks. We operate in the spirit of applied research platforms such as NAMI: not publication alone, but making technology usable, protectable and partner-ready.

2021

於香港成立（前稱納膜科技有限公司），展開納米塗層與生物醫療標靶相關研發。

Incorporated in Hong Kong (formerly Master Masker Technology), beginning nano-coating and biomedical targeting work.

2022

取得三項香港專利授權（護膚組合物相關），奠定配方層知識產權基礎。

Three Hong Kong patents granted (skincare compositions), establishing formulation-layer IP.

2024

公司更名為納米科研技術有限公司；於中國內地提交工藝設備相關專利申請；注資廣州康納科研技術。

Renamed Nanotech Innovation Limited; filed mainland China process-equipment patents; capitalised Guangzhou Kangna.

持續Ongoing

推進 BTRDCC、技術轉移服務與產學研共同開發。

Advancing BTRDCC, technology-transfer services and industry–academia co-development.
